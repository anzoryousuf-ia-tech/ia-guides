Logging in without Microsoft or Google

Your school uses an email and password to log in to Impact Assessment. Here’s how to get your password and sign in for the first time.

1Your school admin invites you
2You set your own password
3You log in with email and password
1

Your school admin sends you an invite

For your school’s Impact Assessment admin

  1. Go to Configuration › School users.
  2. Find the teacher’s row and click the three dots under Actions.
  3. Click Invite. The teacher gets an email to set their password.
The Invited column shows when each person was last sent an invite, so you can check who has been invited.
To invite everyone in the list at once, use Invite all at the top of the page.
If a teacher isn’t in the list, they need to be in your school’s student information system with their school email. Teachers without classes can be added with Add user, then invited.

2

Set your password from the email

For teachers

  1. Open the email from Impact Assessment in your school inbox. If it isn’t there, check your junk or spam folder.
  2. Click the link in the email.
  3. Choose your own password and save it.
No email? You can still set a password yourself. See No invite, or forgot your password.

3

Log in with your email and password

For teachers, every time you log in

  1. Go to app.impactassessment.org.au.
  2. Type your school email address and click Next.
  3. Type the password you set.
  4. Click LOGIN.
Don’t use “Sign in with Microsoft” or “Sign in with Google”. Your school logs in with email and password, so those buttons won’t work for you.
Tick Remember me on your own device so you don’t have to type your email every time.

No invite, or forgot your password

For teachers. You can set a new password yourself.

  1. Go to app.impactassessment.org.au, type your school email and click Next.
  2. On the password screen, click I have forgotten my credentials.
  3. Open the password reset email in your school inbox.
  4. Follow the link and set your new password. Then log in as in step 3.

Still can’t get in? Check these

  • Use your school emailUse the exact school email your school has on record for you, not a personal email.
  • Check junk and spamInvite and reset emails sometimes land there.
  • Use email and passwordNot the Microsoft or Google buttons.
  • Ask your school adminThey can check you’re in the School users list, that your account is active, and send a new invite.
Still stuck after these checks? Your school’s Impact Assessment admin can contact support@impactassessment.org.au.
